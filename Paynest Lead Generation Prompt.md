# Paynest Lead Generation Prompt

**Role:** You are a Web3 Sales Intelligence Agent.

**Objective:** Identify 10 early-stage projects for each region (Europe, USA, and Asia) that fit the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) below.

## ICP Criteria (Strictly Enforced)
- **Industry:** Web3 Ecosystems, DeFi Protocols, GameFi Studios, DAOs, or Blockchain Infrastructure.
- **Team Size:** 3–25 people (focus on lean, scaling teams).
- **Funding Stage:** Recently received a grant, Seed, or Pre-Seed funding in the last 6–12 months.
- **Payment Behavior:** High likelihood of paying a distributed workforce of contributors or contractors in stablecoins (USDC/USDT).
- **Decision Maker:** Founder, COO, or Head of Ops manages the treasury.

## Search Context
- **Europe:** Recent graduates from accelerators like a16z CSX (London) or winners of local hackathons (ETHGlobal, etc.).
- **USA:** Base ecosystem grant winners and early-stage Bitcoin L2 projects.
- **Asia:** Vietnam Web3 Leaders fellowship and winners of Solana/Sui hackathons in Singapore and Vietnam.

## Constraints
- Exclude **red-flag** companies (very mature orgs with massive in-house payroll teams or those that explicitly refuse on-chain payments).

## Key Data Points to Include (ICP Scorecard)
- **Payout Frequency:** Prioritize teams with at least 3 recurring monthly payees or contractors.
- **Multichain Needs:** Identify projects building on multichain infrastructure (e.g., Starknet, Sui, Solana) that require complex off-ramping for their team.
- **Ideal Contacts:** Ecosystem Leads, Treasury Managers, and Founders.

## Output Format (Table)
Provide results in a table with the following columns:

1. **Company Name**
2. **Website URL**
3. **Founder/Key Contact Name**
4. **LinkedIn/X Profile**
5. **One Line Description**
6. **Team Size (Estimate)**
7. **Recent Funding/Grant Detail** (e.g., "Sui Overflow Winner Jan 2026")
8. **Priority Level (High/Medium/Low)** based on scale and funding.

## Deliverable
- **Europe:** 10 projects
- **USA:** 10 projects
- **Asia:** 10 projects

Ensure each entry satisfies all ICP criteria and includes stablecoin payout likelihood, small team size, and recent funding/grant activity.
